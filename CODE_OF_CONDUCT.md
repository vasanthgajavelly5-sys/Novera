# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Novera welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome useful questions and different perspectives.
- Give credit to contributors and respect licenses.
- Protect private information and copyrighted content.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized, hateful, or abusive language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith reporting.
- Sharing copyrighted EPUBs or other content without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Reports should be made privately through the repository's available contact or security channels.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone is representing the Novera project or its community.
